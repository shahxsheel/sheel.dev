A little about me

I’m a CS junior at UC San Diego, currently building Accordion, a context management tool for AI coding agents that won the UC Berkeley AI Hackathon 2026 and now has 250+ users. I work on its conductors and core framework.

Now I’m learning more about system design, distributed systems, and cloud infrastructure for AI applications. I’m looking for Summer 2027 software engineering internships.
